## Trust Boundary Notes

1. **`thread_state_mut()`**: Marked `#[verifier::external]` because Verus cannot express `&mut T` return types. Callers must preserve `wf()`, `spec_id()`, and `spec_alarm()`. Documented with intended postconditions and TODO for future Verus support.

2. **`join_cond()`**: Omitted entirely. `Condvar` is an opaque sync primitive elided from the verification model project-wide. The function is a read-only pass-through with no side effects on `SleepingThread` state. Condvar identity is trivially preserved.

3. **Boundary models**: `ReadyThread` and `InterruptedThread` are local boundary models. Their postconditions carry cross-module verification obligations (documented with `CROSS-MODULE-CHECK` comments and TODOs).

4. **`clock_now()`**: `external_body` boundary with postcondition `result >= 0`. Duplicated from `ready.rs`; TODO to extract to shared module.
