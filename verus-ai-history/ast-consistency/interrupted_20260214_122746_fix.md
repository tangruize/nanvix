The function is read-only and does not affect thread state, identity, or well-formedness. |
| `ReadyThread` (struct) | Documented as justified | Boundary model required to verify the `resume()` state transition. Only `from_state` is modeled — enough to prove identity preservation and interrupt reason propagation across the InterruptedThread → ReadyThread transition. Documented in the module header. |
